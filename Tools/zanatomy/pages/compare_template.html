Options

Recommended

Mix: BodyParts3D bones and muscles + Z-Anatomy fascia

  • Bones and muscles stay credit-only.
  • Share-alike covers only the fascia files we make, which we'd publish openly.
  • Gets the layered skin → fascia → muscle → bone viewer.
  • Cost: more Blender cleanup on the BodyParts3D meshes.
Simplest

Z-Anatomy for everything

  • Cleanest, most complete set; one pipeline.
  • All derived 3D files get published under CC BY-SA.
  • Fine if you're happy for the anatomy assets to be open.
Most permissive

BodyParts3D only

  • Credit-only licence everywhere.
  • No fascia sheets. Fascia would have to be modelled by us or bought.
  • Enough for the Spiral Line figure (its bands are generated, not modelled).

Treating share-alike as covering only the fascia files is the usual reading when separate assets are bundled together. Confirm it if the app will be sold.
