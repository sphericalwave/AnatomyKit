Single-arm low-cable lateral raise

The same exercise, rig and muscle data, drawn two ways. Left: rendered offline in Blender, then looped as video. Right: drawn live in the browser, the way the app's viewer would. Drag the live figure to turn it.

Prime moversAssisting

Both come from one spec (exercises/cable_lateral_raise.json): poses, timing, equipment, camera, and the movement directions to colour. Colours come from AnatomyKit: muscles that are prime movers for shoulder abduction are red; muscles acting in the shoulder blade's upward rotation and elevation are light red. Only the working side is coloured.

Body: Z-Anatomy muscles and bones (CC BY-SA 4.0, Gauthier Kervyn and contributors; built on BodyParts3D, © DBCLS, CC BY 4.0), skinned to the Z-Anatomy rig. First pass: muscles follow their nearest bones, so they stretch but don't bulge; the scapula doesn't rotate yet.
