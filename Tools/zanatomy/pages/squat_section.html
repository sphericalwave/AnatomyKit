Squat test on the Z-Anatomy rig

The back squat already in RigKit, mapped onto Z-Anatomy's own 237-bone rig. Only the 15 Mixamo-style control bones are posed. Vertebrae, ribs, kneecaps and foot bones follow through the rig's constraints, and the knee stays inside the rig's 0–165° limit. Bones only: muscles and fascia aren't rigged yet.
