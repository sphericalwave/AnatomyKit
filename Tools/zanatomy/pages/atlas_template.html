How the line runs

Line routes are a draft reading of Myers, Anatomy Trains (4th ed.), marked unreviewed in AnatomyKit. Lines with several paths (Deep Front, arm lines) are branches or pieces Myers joins through bone. "Crosses" marks where a line passes the midline; with one side selected, tracks after a crossing light on the other side.

Ribbons: each station is placed on its bone by a rule (e.g. the ASIS is the front-most point of the upper hip bone), so stations move rigidly with the skeleton. Where Z-Anatomy has no mesh for a track (the patellar ligament, sternal fascia), the ribbon runs straight between stations.

Meshes: Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0), Gauthier Kervyn and contributors, built on BodyParts3D. Shown at 25% detail. Squat: the Z-Anatomy rig drives the bones; each muscle vertex follows its four nearest bones. That's a first pass, so muscles stretch but don't bulge or slide, and some pass through bone at full depth.
